================================================================================
WHY THIS SPLIT IS SOUND
================================================================================

The repository was GPL-3.0 on the stated grounds that "the pipeline builds on
piper + espeak-ng (both GPLv3)". Half of that is wrong: piper and
piper-phonemize are MIT. The copyleft obligation comes from espeak-ng alone.

The files above were audited for it. They contain no reference to espeak,
piper, or GPL, and no third-party source: the only "verbatim" note in the
runtime is a cross-reference to another file in this same repository. The
espeak-ng code lives entirely in the G2P and port layers, which are not
listed above and stay GPL-3.0.

Two algorithm reimplementations are worth naming, since neither is a copy:

  * mcu/src/snt_nano.c reproduces PyTorch's MT19937 seeding, its 24-bit
    uniform, and normal_fill_16's Box-Muller so that a seeded rendering is
    reproducible across implementations. PyTorch is BSD-3-Clause; this is an
    independent implementation of the documented algorithm, not its source.
  * The decoder mirrors the ConvNeXt-1D and iSTFT-head structure of Vocos and
    the residual-block shape of VITS. Both are MIT. Again structure, not code.

Upstream licences, checked rather than assumed:

  rhasspy/piper             MIT
  rhasspy/piper-phonemize   MIT
  espeak-ng/espeak-ng       GPL-3.0   <- the only copyleft input
  hexgrad/kokoro            Apache-2.0
  gemelo-ai/vocos           MIT
  jaywalnut310/vits         MIT

Model weights are distributed separately from this source, are distilled from
Apache-2.0 (Kokoro) and MIT (piper) teachers, and carry their own terms in
each voice package's meta.json and README.

This is a statement of the project's licensing intent, not legal advice.
